# The Price of Procrastination

We have all heard some version of “Just don’t procrastinate” before. Obviously, no one intends on procrastinating; nobody does it on purpose. The cycle still happens, even when the result is increased stress, rushed work, and regret. Some people manage to get their work done, while others feel trapped in a cycle they do not know how to break.

Everyone has a reason as to why they procrastinate, whether they want to accept it or not. Often, we do not really sit and think about a decision as small as choosing to start a task or put it aside for another time. When we go with the latter, we usually only think about the decision once we are in a state of panic and regret. This is the price associated with procrastination: you either endure the pain of discipline or the pain of regret. For any type of task, the pain of regret is much worse.
