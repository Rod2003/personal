# How to Plan Effectively

At one point, I set a goal to land a new job. The difference between keeping that goal in the back of my mind and giving it dedicated time was obvious. A goal can be acknowledged once in a while, or it can be given enough structure to move forward.

Planning has a limit, too. Applying to every job and reaching out to everyone in a network can become overwhelming. Burnout is real, and it can hold a goal back instead of moving it forward.

The 80/20 rule has always made sense to me: a small part of a task often contains most of what matters. Combined with specific actions, that way of thinking makes progress feel more consistent.
