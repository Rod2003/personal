# Working with Imbalance

We all have our own priorities that vary depending on multiple factors. For example, if I have a product release due tomorrow, a few other tasks to finish, and an invitation to an event tonight, I will prioritize preparing for the product demo.

There is no ideal schedule that everyone must follow; we all have our own concept of what balance looks like in our lives. I do not have much of a work-life balance, and I have found myself working with imbalance when there is a lot of uncertainty in a typical day.

The things that I value most are my personal and professional development, finding avenues to apply learnings, and creating impact for others. My concept of balance is to keep developing myself in these three areas, and most of my time is filled with activities that reflect those priorities.
